## 主题文章

During the **peak season**, a hotel near a famous coastal **destination** welcomed hundreds of **tourists** every week. Most guests completed their **reservation** online before arrival, but they still needed help at the **reception** desk with a **room key card**, a city **map**, and local transport information. To improve **hospitality**, the hotel offered a **breakfast buffet**, an **airport transfer**, and a **guided tour** to nearby **attractions**. Families often booked a **family room**, while business travelers preferred a quiet room near the **conference hall**. Managers discovered that excellent **service** depended on small details, such as clean towels, clear instructions, and fast response to guest requests. In tourism, memorable travel experiences are built not only by scenery, but also by efficient service and thoughtful planning.

## 复习提示

- 旅游文章里要分清 “证件物件词” 和 “酒店服务词”。
- 高频组合：`make a reservation`, `check in`, `visit an attraction`, `book a guided tour`。
- 写作旅游话题时，可以多用 `service`, `destination`, `itinerary`, `hospitality`。
