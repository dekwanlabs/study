## 主题文章

During a large online shopping festival, a major **retailer** received thousands of new **orders** every hour. To keep the system moving, workers in the **warehouse** used a **barcode scanner**, a **sorting belt**, and a **shipping label** system to prepare each **package** quickly. The company promised **same-day delivery** in large cities and **express shipping** for customers in nearby regions. Managers also improved their **tracking** service so buyers could check every stage of **delivery** on the app. Even with these tools, problems still appeared when heavy rain changed the normal **route** of local **couriers**. This is why strong logistics depends on both technology and teamwork. Fast shopping is easy to promise, but reliable fulfillment requires storage, packing, transport, and customer support to work together.

## 复习提示

- 电商阅读里要同时识别 “订单流程词” 和 “仓储设备词”。
- 高频名词建议优先记：`barcode scanner`、`shipping label`、`pallet`、`locker`。
- 文章里常见固定搭配：`place an order`, `track a package`, `request a return`。
