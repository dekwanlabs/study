## 主题文章

A residential community decided to reduce **carbon** **emissions** by investing in a **rooftop solar system** and a shared **battery storage system**. Workers installed each **solar panel**, connected the units through an **inverter**, and linked them to the local **grid** with a **smart meter**. The project also added several **charging stations** for residents who owned an **electric vehicle**. Local officials supported the plan because it could improve energy **conservation**, reduce air **pollution**, and encourage more **sustainable** behavior. However, engineers warned that clean energy is not only about buying new equipment. Cities must also **regulate** building standards, upgrade old power systems, and educate people about efficient energy use. The project showed that environmental protection becomes more effective when technology, public policy, and daily life move in the same direction.

## 复习提示

- 环保类阅读高频出现 `carbon`, `emission`, `sustainable`, `pollution`。
- 设备名优先记：`solar panel`, `wind turbine`, `battery pack`, `charging station`。
- 写作时很好用的搭配：`reduce emissions`, `save energy`, `protect resources`。
