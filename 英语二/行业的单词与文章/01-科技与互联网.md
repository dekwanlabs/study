## 主题文章

At a technology company, the product team planned to launch a new **smartphone** and a matching **mobile app**. Engineers first tested the **chip**, **processor**, and **graphics card** inside the phone to make sure the **hardware** could support new features. Meanwhile, software developers improved the **operating system**, redesigned the user **interface**, and strengthened **security** to protect customer **privacy**. Because millions of users would connect through the **internet**, the company also upgraded its **server** system and expanded **cloud storage**. Managers believed that consumers no longer wanted a single device. They wanted a complete digital experience that connected the phone, watch, and home appliances through one **online platform**. The success of the product, therefore, depended not only on good design, but also on fast networks, stable software, and reliable hardware working together.

## 复习提示

- 优先记住具体设备名，如 `chip`、`processor`、`router`、`server`。
- 阅读科技文章时，先分清 “硬件名词” 和 “软件服务名词”。
- 写作时可以把 `security`、`privacy`、`platform`、`algorithm` 作为高频素材。
