## 主题文章

A community **hospital** recently improved its **outpatient service** to reduce waiting time for each **patient**. At the front desk, nurses first recorded the main **symptom** and arranged a quick **health check**. Doctors then used a **stethoscope**, a **thermometer**, and sometimes an **X-ray machine** to complete the **diagnosis**. If a person needed medicine, the clinic could provide **prescription medicine**, **painkillers**, or **antibiotics** immediately. For more serious cases, the hospital offered **surgery**, **inpatient care**, and long-term **physical therapy**. The director explained that modern health care is not only about treating disease after it appears. It is also about **prevention**, early testing, and helping people return to normal life during **recovery**. This is why even small local clinics need good equipment, trained staff, and efficient service.

## 复习提示

- 医疗英语里最重要的是区分 “症状、设备、服务、药品”。
- `diagnosis`、`treatment`、`recovery` 是文章里经常连在一起出现的逻辑链。
- 写作时可以把 `prevention is better than treatment` 作为观点句来用。
