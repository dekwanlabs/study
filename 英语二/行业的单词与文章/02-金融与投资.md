## 主题文章

When a local bank introduced its new **mobile banking** service, many older customers still preferred to visit a branch in person. They trusted the staff more when opening a **savings account**, applying for a **personal loan**, or asking about an **insurance policy**. To reduce confusion, the bank designed simple guides showing how to use a **bank card**, check a balance at the **ATM**, and read an **invoice** or **receipt** correctly. Financial advisers also explained the difference between a **stock fund**, a **bond fund**, and a low-risk **retirement plan**. Their main message was clear: good financial decisions depend on understanding both **risk** and **return**. Technology can make a **transaction** faster, but careful planning is still necessary if people want to protect their assets and avoid unnecessary debt.

- 再区分常见金融产品，如 `savings account`、`mortgage`、`mutual fund`。
- 阅读财经文章时，看到 `risk`、`return`、`asset`、`debt` 要优先反应出来。
