## 主题文章

An agricultural company worked with a food factory to shorten the **supply chain** from farm to supermarket. On the farm, workers used a **tractor**, a **greenhouse**, and an **irrigation pipe** system to improve the growth of each **crop**. Fresh products were stored in a **cold room** before transport so that quality and **nutrition** could be protected. At the food plant, managers processed different items such as **chicken wing**, **beef**, **yogurt**, **bread**, and **frozen dumplings** for urban consumers. They also promoted **organic** food to customers who cared about health and food safety. The company learned that success in the modern food industry depends not only on farming skills, but also on storage technology, transport efficiency, and clear product standards. From the field to the table, every step matters.

## 复习提示

- 这一页最重要的是把 “农业设备词” 和 “食品名词” 一起记住。
- 食品类写作里可以直接使用 `beef`, `pork`, `milk`, `bread`, `rice`, `sauce`。
- 阅读食品安全和农业现代化文章时，`supply chain`, `storage`, `nutrition`, `organic` 很常见。
