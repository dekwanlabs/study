## 主题文章

In a large **factory**, managers must balance **production** speed with **quality** control. Workers on the **assembly line** use a **conveyor belt**, a **robot arm**, and a **control panel** to produce household products such as a **refrigerator**, a **washing machine**, and an **air conditioner**. Before the goods leave the workshop, inspectors check whether each part meets the required **standard** and whether there is any **defect** in the final product. Regular **maintenance** of machines such as the **forklift**, **lathe**, and **drill** is also essential, because one broken device can slow the entire system. In modern manufacturing, long-term success depends not only on output, but on stable suppliers, safe equipment, and consistent quality.

## 复习提示

- 制造业阅读里要分清 “设备词” 和 “成品词”。
- 高频设备名建议优先记：`assembly line`、`forklift`、`robot arm`、`mold`。
- 写作时可以把 `quality`, `efficiency`, `standard`, `maintenance` 组合成一句论证。
