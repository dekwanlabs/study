## 主题文章

At a modern university, learning no longer takes place only in the **classroom**. Students attend a traditional **lecture**, complete an **assignment**, and also join an **online course** or a small **seminar**. In science departments, they may work in a **laboratory** and use a **microscope** or a **projector** during presentations. Libraries remain important as well, because serious **research** still depends on careful reading and note-taking. To support different needs, universities now offer a wide range of services, including **tutoring service**, **exam preparation**, and even an **internship program**. Good education, therefore, is not simply a collection of tests. It is a system that combines knowledge, tools, teaching methods, and opportunities to help students develop practical ability and academic discipline.

## 复习提示

- 教育类文章高频出现 `curriculum`、`research`、`evaluate`、`graduate`。
- 物件名里重点记 `textbook`、`projector`、`laboratory`、`microscope`。
- 写作教育话题时，可以把 “online course + internship program + research” 组合起来使用。
