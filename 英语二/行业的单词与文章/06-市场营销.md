## 主题文章

A beverage company wanted to improve its **brand** image among young consumers, so it planned a new **campaign** across several media **channels**. First, the team created short videos, a colorful **poster**, and a limited-edition **packaging** design for the product launch. Then they invited online creators to join an **influencer campaign** and shared a daily **social media post** to increase customer **engagement**. After two weeks, the marketing department used a **survey** and direct **feedback** from stores to measure the results. The report showed that the most successful tools were not the most expensive ones. Instead, clear **content**, attractive design, and a well-timed **discount plan** helped the company connect with its target **audience**. The case suggests that good marketing is about understanding people, not simply spending more money.

## 复习提示

- `brand`, `campaign`, `audience`, `engagement` 是营销文章核心词。
- 看到海报、优惠券、包装、视频广告时，要立刻想到营销场景。
- 写作时常用表达：`launch a product`, `promote a brand`, `collect feedback`。
